## Why
Dashboard comparisons mix down payments with fee-only bases and forecasts count entire newly started plans. Managers need consistent period results, debt exposure and scheduled receipts.
## What Changes
- Align comparisons, schedule forecasts and outstanding installment amounts.
- Show expenses, aging, future receipts, concentration and operating ratios.
- Hide firm results under dimension filters without an expense allocation rule.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `dashboard-financial-analysis`: Consistent period metrics and decision support.
### Modified Capabilities
None.
## Impact
Dashboard slice, canonical domain docs and tests. Existing firm and role scopes remain mandatory.
## Non-goals
Bank reconciliation, payout settlement tracking, expense allocation and database changes.
